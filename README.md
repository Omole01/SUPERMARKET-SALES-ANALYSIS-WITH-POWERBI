# SUPERMARKET-SALES-ANALYSIS-WITH-POWERBI
This dashboard shows how the supermarket is performing across sales, customers and products.

The business generated over 308K in total sales, with a steady increase over time, showing growing customer demand. Some product lines perform better than others and contribute the most to revenue, meaning the business should focus more on these high-performing categories.

Sales are also stronger in certain cities, indicating better market performance in those locations. Expanding successful strategies from these cities can help improve sales in other areas.

Customers use different payment methods, but some are clearly more preferred, which can help the business improve customer convenience. Also, members appear to contribute more consistently, showing that loyalty programs are effective.

In summary, the business is performing well and by focusing on top products, key locations, and customer preferences, it can further increase sales and profitability.
